## 1. Executive Summary & Commercial Thesis

GAP FOR GOOD (GFG 2.0) operates as a **dual-sided social enterprise platform**. Its commercial foundation rests on a high-integrity, multi-stakeholder model:

1. **Beneficiary-Centered Free Core:** Core diagnostic tools, learning modules, confidence-building activities, community comeback circles, and the verifiable Career Passport are **100% free of charge for returning women**. GFG strictly rejects predatory "pay-to-work" or placement-fee models charged to vulnerable candidates.
2. **B2B Employer Value Capture:** Corporate employers pay for verified recruiter access, structured returnship builder toolkits, skills-first candidate discovery, and cohort pipeline management.
3. **B2G & Institutional CSR Sponsorships:** Government skilling initiatives and corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) foundations fund dedicated returner cohorts with verifiable, de-identified macro reporting.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Commercial Integrity Notice:**
> GAP FOR GOOD is currently at the pre-revenue, software-complete prototype stage. All revenue figures, subscription tiers, pricing assumptions, and unit economics in this document represent **commercial hypotheses formulated for empirical validation during upcoming pilot programs**, not historic or realized commercial transactions.

## 3. Jobs-To-Be-Done (JTBD) Analysis

Applying the Christensen Jobs-To-Be-Done framework clarifies why stakeholders adopt GFG:

### 3.1 Returning Women (Beneficiaries)
- **Functional Job:** "Help me refresh my modern technical currency, prove my competence objectively, and find an employer who won't discard my resume because of my career break."

- **Functional Job:** "Deliver structured, high-completion technical skilling programs to women with verifiable graduation milestones."

## 9. Ethical Monetization Principles

To prevent mission drift and ensure perpetual alignment with women's empowerment, GFG commits to four inviolable ethical guardrails:

1. **Zero Fees on Returning Candidates:** The platform will never charge candidates for job discovery, passport export, mentor bookings, or application submissions.
2. **No Guaranteed Placement Guarantees:** GFG will never charge candidates upfront fees for "guaranteed employment," avoiding deceptive trade practices common in vocational skilling.
3. **No Pay-To-Rank Employer Listings:** Employers cannot pay extra fees to artificially boost job ranking or bypass the mandatory Caregiving Dignity Charter compliance review.
4. **Data Privacy Over Monetization:** Candidate data will never be sold to third-party data brokers, advertising networks, or unverified recruiters.

## 10. Commercial Risks & Mitigation Strategies

1. **Risk: Slow Employer Adoption of Structured Returnships**
   - *Mitigation:* Provide ready-to-use returnship framework templates, HR toolkits, and structured legal rubrics (`backend/app/routers/employer_intelligence.py`), reducing organizational setup overhead from months to minutes.
2. **Risk: B2B Long Sales Cycles**
   - *Mitigation:* Offer lightweight self-service "Verified Recruiter Desk" tiers with immediate verification, avoiding prolonged enterprise procurement processes for pilot cohorts.
3. **Risk: High Infrastructure Costs from Generative AI**
   - *Mitigation:* Multi-tier AI architecture prioritizing deterministic local heuristic engines and caching, invoking external LLM APIs only when strictly required for unstructured semantic transformations.
